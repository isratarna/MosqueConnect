# Mosque Connect – Web Application Overview

Mosque Connect is a web application designed to help users easily discover mosques based on their specific preferences while also strengthening community engagement through donations, volunteering, announcements, and support services.

## 1. Browse Mosques

The Browse Mosques page is the core feature of the application. It allows users to find mosques that match their preferred criteria.

Users can search and filter mosques using various options, such as location, available facilities, services, or other preferences. The results can be displayed in two different formats:

### List View

Displays all matching mosques along with essential information. From here, users can:

- View detailed information about a mosque.
- Get directions using maps.
- Follow a mosque to receive future updates.

### Map View

Users can switch to an interactive map where all matching mosques are displayed as map markers, making it easier to locate nearby mosques visually.

---

## 2. Support

The Support page is dedicated to helping users contribute to mosques and the surrounding community in different ways. It consists of five support options:

### Money Donation

Users can donate directly to a mosque by selecting:

- The mosque they wish to support.
- The purpose of the donation.
- The donation amount.

After submitting the information, they are redirected to a secure payment gateway to complete the transaction.

### Blood Donation

This section displays a live feed of active blood requests submitted by mosques or community members.

Users can:

- Browse ongoing blood requests.
- Register themselves as blood donors for future emergencies.

### Volunteer

This page lists current volunteering opportunities from different mosques, such as community events, charity activities, and mosque services.

Users can also register themselves as volunteers so they can be contacted whenever volunteer assistance is needed.

### Goods Donation

Mosques can publish requests for essential goods they currently need.

Users can browse these requests using filters such as:

- Nearby
- Urgent
- Category

They can then choose the requested items they wish to donate.

### Custom Support

If users want to contribute in ways other than donations, they can submit a custom support request.

The form allows users to specify the type of support they want to provide, such as:

- Sponsoring an event
- Teaching Islamic or educational classes
- Sponsoring an orphan
- Any other community initiative

An additional description field allows users to explain their proposal in detail.

---

## 3. Community

The Community page serves as a centralized hub for updates and announcements from mosques.

It includes:

- Mosque announcements
- Active blood requests
- Volunteer opportunities
- Prayer schedule updates
- Mosque notices
- Community updates and other important announcements

This page ensures that users can stay informed about everything happening within their local mosque community.

---

## 4. Home Page

The Home page provides users with a quick overview of the platform and its key features.

It includes:

### Introduction Section

A brief description explaining the purpose and mission of Mosque Connect.

### Interactive Nearby Mosque Map

Upon entering the website, users are asked for location permission. The homepage then displays an interactive map centered around their current location.

### Nearest Mosque Highlight

Alongside the map, the application showcases the nearest or most relevant mosque with important information and quick access to its details.

### Support the Community

A dedicated section encouraging users to contribute through donations, volunteering, or other forms of support.

### Our Impact

Displays the platform's community impact, such as the number of mosques connected, donations made, volunteers registered, or people helped.

### About Us & Contact

Provides information about the project along with contact details for users who wish to get in touch.

---

## 5. User Account

Before logging in, users have access to:

- Login
- Register

After logging in, these options are replaced with:

- Profile
- Notifications

The profile allows users to manage their personal information, while the notification system keeps them updated on mosque announcements, volunteer opportunities, donation requests, and other activities from the mosques they follow.

## 6. User Roles and Mosque Profiles

Mosque Connect supports four different user roles, each with specific permissions and responsibilities.

### Visitor

Visitors are users who have not logged in. They can browse the platform with limited access to public information, such as mosque listings and general announcements.

### User

Registered users can fully interact with the platform. They can:

- Search and filter mosques.
- View detailed mosque profiles.
- Follow mosques to receive updates.
- Donate money, blood, goods, or volunteer.
- Register as a blood donor or volunteer.
- Receive notifications from the mosques they follow.

### Mosque Admin

Each registered mosque has its own official profile, managed by one or more Mosque Admins.

Mosque Admins can:

- Manage and update their mosque's profile.
- Publish announcements and community updates.
- Post blood requests, volunteer opportunities, and goods requests.
- Update prayer schedules and other important information.

Announcements and updates published by Mosque Admins are automatically displayed on the Community page for users who follow the mosque or browse community updates.

### Super Admin

The Super Admin oversees the entire platform and ensures its authenticity and security.

Their responsibilities include:

- Verifying mosque profiles before they become publicly available.
- Approving or rejecting mosque registration requests.
- Monitoring Mosque Admin activities.
- Maintaining the overall integrity and reliability of the platform.

This verification process ensures that users interact only with authentic and trusted mosque profiles.
